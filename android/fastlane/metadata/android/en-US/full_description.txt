Rokko is a free and open source chess app that also works offline. No ads, no accounts, no subscriptions and no data collected.

Play
• against the computer (Stockfish on your phone) with 8 levels, bots with personality, a coach and variants;
• against a friend on the same phone, over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or remotely, with encrypted moves;
• with a clock, zen mode, a physical board mode and handicap games.

Learn
• puzzles of every kind, an openings explorer and repertoire, endgames and master games;
• a stepped, story-driven path, also made for children, with a dedicated profile;
• review and analysis of your own games, with coach comments.

Make it yours
• piece sets, boards, backgrounds and sounds to choose from, light or dark theme, colors for color-blind players;
• a game archive with PGN import and export, backup and restore;
• Italian and English, with screen reader support and adjustable text.

Rokko is free software (GPL-3.0 or later): the code is at https://github.com/diegociarafoni/rokko. Your data stays on your phone.
